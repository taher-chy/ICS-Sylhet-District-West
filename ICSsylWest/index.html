In Memory of the 99th Shaheed 99th Shaheed Martyrdom: 1 January 1999

Shaheed Muhammad Enamul Haque Dudu

The 99th Shaheed of Bangladesh Islami Chhatrashibir, Shaheed Muhammad Enamul Haque Dudu, hailed from the Tajpur area of Osmaninagar Upazila (then Balaganj), Sylhet.

🏡

Birth & Family

  • Date of Birth: 19 January 1972.
  • Birthplace: Khashipara village of Tajpur Union under then Balaganj (now Osmaninagar) Thana.
  • Parents & Family: Father late Muhammad Wahab. He was the third among two brothers and three sisters.
🎓

Educational Life

  • Passed Dakhil with distinction in 1992 from Chandarai Para Dakhil Madrasa, Balaganj.
  • Subsequently enrolled in Alim at Bishwanath Alia Madrasa.
  • For higher studies, he studied at Tajpur Degree College and completed Kamil while pursuing his 2nd year of Bachelor (Degree).
🕊️

Chhatrashibir & Martyrdom

  • He was a dedicated "Member" (Sadasya) of Bangladesh Islami Chhatrashibir.
  • According to organizational records of Chhatrashibir, he is the 99th Shaheed of the organization.
  • His martyrdom is recorded on 1 January 1999 amid the prevailing political circumstances. Every year, Sylhet District West and Tajpur Degree College branches organize memorial meetings, doa mahfils, and discussion programs in his honor.
